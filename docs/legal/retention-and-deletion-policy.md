# Retention and Deletion Policy

Version: 1.0
Status: Final Policies
Effective date: 10 August 2026

Internal/operational policy — not published as a public page (unlike Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, and Acceptable Use Policy, which are live at `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/cookies`, `/acceptable-use`). Available on request per the Privacy Policy.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how long we keep personal data and how we delete or anonymise it when it is no longer needed.

## 2. Retention principle

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to meet legal obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our rights.

## 3. Retention categories

The following categories should be reviewed and assigned specific retention periods:

- account and profile data
- group membership records
- contribution and governance records
- support requests and correspondence
- security logs
- billing and accounting records
- legal hold / dispute records

## 4. Suggested operational approach

Unless counsel advises otherwise:

- keep active account data while the account remains open
- review inactive account data after a defined inactivity period
- retain support records only as long as needed for service management
- retain security logs only as long as needed for monitoring and incident response
- retain accounting records in line with tax and bookkeeping obligations
- retain legal dispute records until the matter is fully resolved

## 5. Deletion and anonymisation

When data is no longer needed, we will:

- delete it securely
- anonymise it where appropriate; or
- archive it where retention is required by law or for legitimate business reasons

## 6. User deletion requests

Where a user requests deletion, we will assess whether the data can be deleted immediately or whether legal or operational reasons require retention.

## 7. Reviews

This policy should be reviewed regularly and whenever the product, law, or processing activities change.
