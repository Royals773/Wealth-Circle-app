# Data Processing Addendum

Version: 1.0
Status: Final Policies
Effective date: 10 August 2026

Internal/operational policy — not published as a public page. This is the document typically shared directly with a customer/group entering into a specific processing arrangement, not surfaced as a general marketing-site page.

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between [Controller name] and WealthCircle.

## 1. Roles

The parties will identify in the main agreement whether WealthCircle acts as a processor, controller, or independent controller for each processing activity.

Where WealthCircle processes personal data on documented instructions from the customer, WealthCircle acts as a processor.

## 2. Subject matter and duration

This DPA applies to the processing of personal data necessary to provide the WealthCircle platform and related support services for the duration of the main agreement.

## 3. Nature and purpose of processing

The nature and purpose of processing may include:

- account administration
- group membership management
- contribution and governance record-keeping
- support and troubleshooting
- security monitoring
- backup and recovery

## 4. Categories of data

The categories of data may include:

- names
- contact information
- dates of birth
- residential addresses
- next-of-kin details
- account identifiers
- contribution and governance records
- log and audit data

## 5. Categories of data subjects

The categories of data subjects may include:

- group members
- administrators
- account holders
- support contacts
- emergency contacts provided by users

## 6. Instructions

WealthCircle will process personal data only on documented instructions from the controller, except where required by law.

## 7. Confidentiality

WealthCircle will ensure that persons authorised to process personal data are subject to confidentiality obligations.

## 8. Security

WealthCircle will implement appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data.

## 9. Sub-processors

WealthCircle may use sub-processors to provide the service, subject to appropriate contractual safeguards.

## 10. Assistance

WealthCircle will reasonably assist the controller with:

- data subject requests
- security incidents and breach response
- compliance obligations related to the processing

## 11. Deletion or return

At the end of the service, WealthCircle will delete or return personal data as agreed, unless retention is required by law.

## 12. Audits and information

WealthCircle will provide reasonable information necessary to demonstrate compliance and, where agreed, permit audits subject to appropriate limitations.

## 13. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred internationally, the parties will ensure a lawful transfer mechanism is in place.

## 14. Order of precedence

If there is any conflict between this DPA and the main agreement regarding data protection matters, this DPA will prevail to the extent of the conflict.
